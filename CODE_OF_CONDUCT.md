# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want F1 Race Intelligence to be a welcoming, respectful, and technically rigorous project for people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Welcome questions from newcomers.
- Give credit for ideas, code, data, and research.
- Keep technical discussions focused on the project.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized behavior, spam, and intentionally misleading data or performance claims are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report a conduct concern privately to the maintainer at [dhyeyteraiya@gmail.com](mailto:dhyeyteraiya@gmail.com). Please include links or context where possible. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled as fairly as possible.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code.
